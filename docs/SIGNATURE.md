# Dělení buňky — signature (draft, not yet reviewed by Pavol)

An explainer, not a registered flywheel product. This file records which classics shaped it,
so the moves can be judged and, if approved, generalized into a standard for explainers.

## Lineage

| Source | Year | Changes |
|---|---|---|
| Comenius, *Orbis sensualium pictus* | 1658 | Parts carry numerals in the picture; the same numeral names the part in the text. No floating labels. |
| Séguin / Montessori, the three-period lesson | 1846 / 1912 | The tabs are the lesson: Prohlížej (this is…), Najdi (show me…), Pojmenuj (what is this?). |
| Montessori, control of error | 1912 | No score, no streak. In Najdi the model names where you actually are; in Seřaď your own order is played back and the jump in the story is the error. |
| Montessori, isolation of difficulty | 1912 | One part can be followed alone: layers switch off, a tapped chromosome keeps its colour while the rest fade. |
| Socratic question | — | Every phase ends in one question whose answer is the next phase; the answer is hidden until asked for. |
| Humanist *ad fontes* | — | Each term comes with its Greek or Latin root; the model is drawn the way Flemming saw it in 1882 (stained preparation, the star from the pole). |
| Roediger & Karpicke, retrieval practice; Richland, Kornell & Kao, pretesting | 2006 / 2009 | Recall steps exist at all; a missed item returns two turns later. |

## Refused

Scores, streaks, badges, confetti, mascots. Labels that cover the model. A wall of text on
first sight. Anything running a model on our side.

## Moves (all `proposed`; built at Pavol's direct request 2026-11-10, pending his review)

1. **Numerals, not labels.** Tap a numeral: the camera goes to that part and a card names it.
2. **Three depths.** First sight: phase name and one sentence. One tap: what happens, counts, the word's origin, parts. Inside a part: "Víc do hloubky" and sub-parts (a, b, c) that only exist once zoomed in.
3. **Four steps of the lesson** in the navigation: Prohlížej, Najdi, Pojmenuj, Seřaď.
4. **The material corrects.** Wrong place is named, wrong order is played.
5. **Two ways of seeing** the same cell: stained preparation (1882) and fluorescence (today).

## Visual signature

A lithographic plate: paper ground, carmine and Prussian blue for the two parental sets
(carmine was an actual 19th-century chromosome stain), sepia spindle, ink outlines from a
rim-darkening shader. Fraunces for names and numerals, Figtree for text.
